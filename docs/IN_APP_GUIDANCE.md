# In-app guidance — v0.6.0

## What changed

The main consultation, check-in and planner now provide explanations, practical steps, scripts and follow-up questions within Sukoon. Resource cards lead first to original Sukoon guidance. Original publisher books and websites remain optional secondary links, preserving the existing library. No iframe, embedded remote service, paid API or live AI backend is used.

The new thirteen-domain guidance covers emotions; social/family pressure; crisis aftermath; money stress; parenting; relationships; recovery; elder care; career; grief; fertility; body shaming/racism; and spiritual questions. A selected mode can add a voluntary conversation structure. This is a self-guided outline, not an accredited mediator, clinician or live consultation.

## What determines a response

The rules use selected topic/domain, response mode and explicit flags for persistence, coercion, a minor's involvement, immediate danger, medical urgency and optional faith comfort. They do not read a free-text account or infer hidden facts. They cannot establish motives, diagnose, decide legal guilt or guarantee outcomes. Full detailed responses are currently English; the existing multilingual planner and critical labels are retained. Independent clinical and translation reviews remain pending.

Immediate danger or a medical emergency suppresses ordinary advice and mediation, and displays human-help guidance inside the app. Coercion, minors, parenting and crisis selections suppress the joint-mediation outline. Personal support remains available without making users negotiate their safety. No crisis numbers are hardcoded.

## Privacy and offline use

The consultation module makes no network or storage calls. Consultation selections and reports stay in memory, are not written to the optional journal, and are not included in journal exports. Clear consultation and Clear all data remove the rendered report. The release-scoped offline shell includes the guidance module. An initial successful online shell installation is still required.

Publisher links are optional and require internet when opened. Core explanations and actions require no publisher visit. Original books have not been copied, rehosted, automatically summarised or presented as Sukoon-authored books. The original prompts, runtime guidance and other tools remain intact.

## Accuracy boundary

These are original pre-written educational guides, not individual professional assessment. Every possible life situation cannot be covered accurately by a finite rule set. Significant, persistent or urgent concerns may still need appropriate real-world human support; an app cannot substitute for emergency or medical care. No claim of 100% accuracy, clinical validation or professional sign-off is made.

## Verification

New tests exercise all thirteen domains across three modes, danger/medical overrides, restricted mediation, faith opt-in, persistence flags, resource routing, malformed choices, no network/storage APIs and actual simulated UI response flows. Existing tests are retained, with SW version expectations advanced to this release.

A local execution environment was unavailable for this iteration. Validation must be read from GitHub's test and deployment runs; do not substitute static syntax inspection for the test result. Real-device, assistive-technology, native-speaker and clinical reviews are still not completed.
