# Independent review record and handoff — v0.5.0

## Status

**No independent clinical, translation or community review has been completed or arranged with a named person.** No reviewer has been contacted through this session. The app is not clinically approved, professionally translated or certified culturally appropriate. v0.5.0 remains a pre-production release.

The following handoff is ready for reviewers. It is a review plan, not a completed review outcome. Do not enter real user disclosures in review examples.

## Mental-health professional review

Reviewer needed: a qualified mental-health professional familiar with South Asian and diaspora communities. Verify credentials and relevant scope before recording approval. Review `behaviour.mjs`, `planner.mjs`, `i18n.mjs`, urgent routing in `core.mjs`, safety/UI language, the locked master prompt and runtime guidance. No diagnosis, legal verdict or guaranteed safety should be implied.

Check affected-person, witness and own-behaviour perspectives; all 45 topic/role/setting combinations; uncertain patterns; power imbalance; minors; eating concerns; immediate danger; faith opt-in; body neutrality; pressure to confront; and referral wording. Review the actual rendered flows as well as source text.

Record reviewer name/role, independently verified qualifications, date, exact commit/files, conflicts of interest, concerns, recommended edits, dispositions and retest evidence. Scope any sign-off to the reviewed version and intended use; do not label the entire app clinically validated.

Current outcome: **Pending; no sign-off.**

## Native Urdu and Roman Urdu review

Reviewer needed: an independent native speaker or professional translator with mental-health communication experience. Review every string in `planner.mjs` and `i18n.mjs`, including safety language, options, plural/gender phrasing, readability, respectful register and Urdu-script rendering. Compare meaning with the English source; test mixed English/Urdu screen-reader pronunciation.

Record credentials or linguistic background, date, commit, each reviewed string/key, proposed change, rationale and final acceptance. Do not treat AI-generated translations or an automated script check as a native-speaker review.

Current outcome: **Pending; translations are AI-drafted and unverified independently.**

## Community/cultural review

Invite voluntary feedback from adults with varied South Asian and UK-diaspora experiences. Include different literacy levels and family/work contexts without requiring anyone to disclose personal trauma. Use fictional scenarios and let participants skip any question.

Ask whether wording feels respectful, whether reporting/confrontation suggestions feel safe in the scenario, whether faith opt-in is clear, whether colourism is understood, and whether the starting routes reduce confusion. Obtain consent before recording feedback; keep identifying information out of this public repository. Summarise observations, disagreements, limitations and resulting changes without claiming population-wide validation.

Current outcome: **Pending; no participant sessions or findings.**

## Release gate

These independent reviews and actual device/assistive-technology testing are required before claiming the relevant gaps are closed. Until evidence exists, retain this pending status and the transparent product limitations.
