    Session only.

    You deserve human support, too.

    If you are in immediate danger, seek local emergency help now or ask a trusted person nearby to help you reach it. This app cannot monitor your safety or contact services for you. Use a safer device if someone is monitoring yours.

    If distress persists, worsens or interferes with daily life, speak with a qualified mental health professional. Self-help can complement that support.

    Install and verify on your own device

    Android, iPhone and offline steps (English)
    1. Use the repository’s verified GitHub Pages HTTPS address. In Android Chrome, choose Install app / Add to Home screen where available. In iPhone Safari, choose Share → Add to Home Screen.
    2. Open the installed app online once and wait until the app reports that offline files are ready. Then enable airplane mode, close and reopen it, and check the planner, calming tools and saved reflections.
    3. Publisher books need internet access; they are not downloaded into the app.
    4. For a waiting update, finish/export unsaved work, close all Sukoon tabs and reopen. The app never forces an update during your reflection.
    5. Try keyboard navigation, 200% zoom, reduced motion, high contrast and your screen reader. A complete verified test record has not yet been produced for these devices.
